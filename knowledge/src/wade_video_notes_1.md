Recurring vocabulary (stated across videos, consolidated here so each section can reference it):
- Key entry point (KEP): trendline / channel line, support or resistance (key level), or the 21-bar EMA. "Two key entry points" = EMA plus trendline (or level) coinciding.
- High probability setup (HPS): two-legged pullback = second entry with the trend at a KEP; failed second entry against the trend; higher-low / lower-high confirmation; failed breakout (fade); double top/bottom; "double trap" (trapped traders at two KEPs, called "a little more advanced").
- Trendline rule: after a break of a trendline/channel, expect at least a correction and most likely ("90%", "8 or 9 times out of 10") a retest / break to a new extreme. Only after the new extreme forms does he look for correction or reversal.
- Entry trigger: 1 tick beyond the signal bar (above high for longs, below low for shorts). Stop: 1 tick beyond the other side of the signal bar. Never moved.
