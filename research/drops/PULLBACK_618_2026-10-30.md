# Pullback-to-61.8% rule (fib_causal.py), 2026-10-30

Leg = move of >= 60 pts on 1m: A = highest high (lowest low for longs) of the last 120 bars, B = lowest low after it.
Setup when price bounces >= 30% of the leg off B, AND the 5m EMA21 (built from 1m) slopes in the leg's direction.
Entry: limit at B + 0.618 x leg (shorts) / B - 0.618 x leg (longs), valid 60 min or until a new low/high beyond B.
Stop: 2 pts beyond A (the lower high / higher low). Target: B (the leg's low/high). Time stop 60 min. Cost 1 pt.
Causal: everything computed from closed bars (an earlier version had a look-ahead in leg selection: discarded).
