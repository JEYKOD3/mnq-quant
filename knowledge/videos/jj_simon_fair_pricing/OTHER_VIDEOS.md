## Fair price
- **Conflict across videos:**
  - The 9:29 video: the fair price is the body of the 9:29 candle, drawn as a zone.
  - The podcast: the 9:30 opening price.
  - The $1.2M video: says "opening price", but marks the pre-open candle as a box.
- **News days:** the fair price is the body of the 8:29 candle.
- **Evening (6 PM reopen):** the fair price is the 6 PM open. The continuation leans toward filling the gap from the prior close. Sunday opens are fairer than weekday ones.
- **Asia (8 PM):** usually trades back toward the 6 PM price. If Asia opens near the 6 PM price, there's no continuation trade.

## Sessions he trades
6:00 PM reopen, 8:00 PM Asia, 3:00 AM London, 8:30 AM news, 9:30 AM New York, 2:00 PM New York PM.

## Trade sequence per session
- **Continuation:** only in the first 0-10 minutes. Reversion trades after that.
- **Trade limit:** 3-4 trades per session at most. If they're losing, the bias isn't working, so he stops.
- **Re-entry:** fine right after a stop-out if a new displacement appears.
- **End:** stops at 11:00, or earlier if volume dies.
- **Quiet sessions** (Asia, NY PM, quiet Mondays): same rules on the 5-minute chart.

## Entry grades
- **A+:** displacement plus a break-of-structure close, with the reversion target and the short-term continuation agreeing.
- **A:** displacement only.
- **B:** the two biases disagree. He skips these.
- **Displacement:** the $1.2M video defines it as bigger than the previous candle with little or no wick. The podcast uses body size, close beyond the wick, and opposite colour.
- **Room to target:** there must be points "in your favour". Don't enter when price is already at fair price.

## News
- **Which releases:** US red-folder releases at 8:30 only (Forex Factory).
- **In line with forecast:** revert to the 8:29 body.
- **Big surprise:** the latest consolidation becomes the new fair price, and he trades a continuation during the first candle.
- **Avoid:** being in a trade at 9:45 or 10:00 releases. Be careful around the 9:30 open (widen the stop or move to breakeven).
- **One reversion:** take the first reversion only; the edge fades with volume.
- He calls this his highest win-rate trade and risks more on it.

- **Account ratios** (target ÷ max loss): Lucid 25K is 1:1.25, 50K is 1:1.5, 150K is 1:2. The best risk/reward differs by account size.
