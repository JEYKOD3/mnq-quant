### A4. Geopolitics and politics

- US-Israel war on Iran began Feb 28, 2026; an Apr 8 ceasefire was announced but Hormuz traffic never normalized. No ceasefire is reported in force now; Qatar mediating in Tehran (Sun Oct 25). [BRecorder], [Wikipedia]
- Houthis struck a Saudi airport and oil facilities (Sat Oct 24); Saudi intercepted more missiles Sun. Bab el-Mandeb and Hormuz shipping disrupted. [BRecorder]
- Trump: "getting to be decision time" on a deal vs resuming attacks; has suggested strikes could resume after the Nov 3 midterms. US is reducing staff in parts of the region. Newsquawk carried a headline that the US could strike "as soon as the weekend." [EdgeMY], [Newsquawk]
- US midterms Tue Nov 3 (next week). Democrats favoured to flip the House; Senate too close to call. [Newsquawk]
- Israel Knesset election Tue Oct 27. China Fifth Plenum Oct 26 to 29. Canada tariff changes (Section 338) effective Oct 30. [Newsquawk]
- BofA's Hartnett: "tactically risk-off until mid-Nov." [EdgeMY]
